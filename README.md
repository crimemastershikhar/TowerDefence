# 🏰 **Tower Defense Game** 🎯

## 🧠 **Overview**

**Tower Defense** is a strategic 3D game built using **Unity** where your objective is to protect your base from waves of advancing enemies by deploying and upgrading defensive turrets.

Enemies follow a fixed path, and you must tactically place turrets to eliminate them before they reach the endpoint.

---

## 🛡️ **Key Feature – Turret Defense System**

The **core mechanic and standout feature** of this game is its **dynamic Turret Defense System**:

- 🌀 **Multiple Turret Types** – Basic shooter, sniper, and missile turrets with varying stats.
- 🎯 **Auto-Targeting System** – Turrets intelligently detect and lock onto enemies within range.
- 🔄 **Rotating Turrets** – Real-time enemy tracking and turret rotation for immersive feel.
- 💣 **Strategic Placement** – Build turrets only on designated build nodes to encourage tactical thinking.

---

## ⚔️ **Gameplay Mechanics**

- Enemies follow a fixed path to your base.
- You earn points for each enemy destroyed.
- Each wave becomes more challenging with faster and tougher enemies.
- Survive as many waves as you can to achieve a high score.
